Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026. P03 task bot is a personal app used only by its owner, Israel Indursky. No other person's Google account is connected to it.

Google data the app accesses

  • Google Calendar events of the owner's primary calendar (read, create and move events).
  • Gmail messages of the owner (search and read sender, subject, date and up to 2,000 characters of text), and Gmail drafts (create a draft, and send it only when the owner taps a send button twice).

How the data is used

  • Only to answer the owner's own requests in his private Telegram chat with the bot, and to list his calendar events in his morning summary.
  • To understand a request, the relevant text (for example the day's events or a mail the owner asked about) is sent to Anthropic's Claude API, which writes the answer. It is not used to train AI models.
  • Google data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

Storage and security

  • The app runs on the owner's own computer. The Google sign-in token is stored there in a local file and is never published.
  • The app keeps a log of the owner's chat with the bot, including the results of the calendar and mail lookups he asked for, in a local database on that same computer. It is not uploaded anywhere else.
  • The owner can disconnect the app at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Limited Use

P03 task bot's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
