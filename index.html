P03 task bot

P03 task bot is a private Telegram assistant built by Israel Indursky for his own personal use. It is not offered to the public, and it answers only one Telegram account: its owner's.

The bot keeps the owner's tasks, notes, debts and reminders. With the owner's permission it also connects to his own Google account to:

  • show his Google Calendar events when he asks what is planned for a day, and in a daily morning summary;
  • create or move a calendar event, only after he taps a confirm button in the chat;
  • search and read his Gmail messages, only when he asks;
  • prepare a Gmail draft after he taps a confirm button, and send it only after two more taps.
